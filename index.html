Calibrate the phone to the wall

Send a gray step pattern from the LED processor or media server straight to the wall, bypassing cameras. Tap each step, then the color bars. This removes the phone's own exposure, tone curve and color boost from the readings.

The test pattern is full-range RGB. Play it out the same way as the show feeds: same media server output, same full or legal range setting, 100% scale, no color correction. Otherwise 0% and 100% land in the wrong place and every reading shifts.

It only holds for the picture it was tapped on, or in live mode while exposure stays locked.

Where these come from: chip levels are the chart makers' published values or the Rec.709 camera curve. Skin ranges are common shading practice. The EBU R103 range drawn on the waveform is the only formal standard, and it can't be verified through a camera. Balance, evenness, clipping, crush and gamut-edge limits are this tool's own working tolerances.
